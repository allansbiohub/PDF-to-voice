Mandatory Distinction: Background Audio vs. Background AI Compute

1. Audio Playback Continuing in Background (Supported on iPhone): iOS Safari and Home-Screen Web Apps allow an active HTML5 <audio> element to continue playing indefinitely while the screen is locked, exposing Lock-Screen and Control Center transport buttons via navigator.mediaSession.

2. Foreground JS / LLM / TTS Inference in Background (Throttled by iOS): iOS suspends WebAssembly/WebGPU threads and JavaScript timers ~5–30 seconds after locking the screen. Relying on per-sentence JS callbacks fails after one sentence.

3. Our Engineering Solution: VoxLibro pre-synthesizes a rolling multi-paragraph window (or full chapter) into a single stitched .wav audio stream with embedded timecode boundaries. When you lock your iPhone and put it in your pocket, iOS plays the continuous .wav buffer uninterrupted and maps audio.currentTime directly back to your exact sentence offset.
